# VOICE PROFILE

Author: Kirole product brand

Goal: App Store product-page copy that explains the iPhone app, paired E-Ink device, and companion experience without sounding like a task manager
Confidence: High

## Source Set

- Current Kirole landing page
- `docs/positioning-narrative.md`
- Current privacy policy
- Current companion and focus product rules

## Rhythm

- Short opening lines followed by concrete feature details.
- Calm pacing. One idea per sentence.
- Companion language can be warm; compatibility and privacy language stays literal.

## Compression

- Lead with the product relationship, then explain how it works.
- Keep screenshot headlines to one promise each.
- Use lists for sources, privacy facts, and hardware requirements.

## Capitalization

- Conventional sentence case in prose.
- Short all-caps section labels are acceptable in the English description.

## Parentheticals

- Avoid them in marketing copy.
- Use them only when a compatibility qualification cannot be stated cleanly in a separate sentence.

## Question Use

- Avoid rhetorical questions and bait.

## Claim Style

- Name the mechanism: connected calendar and task sources, paired E-Ink device, continuous 30-minute focus blocks, and on-device photo processing.
- State hardware and firmware requirements beside hardware claims.
- Separate local photo processing from AI generation and classification, which may send relevant schedule context to the AI provider.

## Preferred Moves

- “Let your day live beside you.”
- Quiet, companion-led language.
- Concrete contrasts with the phone: fewer pings, less screen attention, a day kept in view.

## Banned Moves

- Productivity guilt, streak pressure, or “do more” language.
- Claims that no data ever leaves the phone.
- Unverified battery life, firmware behavior, or unsupported integrations.
- Subscription or pricing promises.
- Generic launch hype.

## CTA Rules

- App Store copy should close with compatibility, not a sales command.

## Channel Notes

- App Store: clear product relationship, verified feature list, privacy facts, compatibility.
- Screenshot captions: one short emotional promise per image.
- Website: may explain the day-in-the-life story in more detail.
