Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 15, 2026 · Applies to the Kirole iPhone app, the Kirole E-ink companion device, and this website.

The short version: Kirole keeps calendar and task content on your iPhone and paired device rather than storing that content on our servers. When you use connected services, local weather, or AI writing, the app sends the information needed for that request to the relevant service. Our backend stores account, companion, and sync metadata. We show no ads, embed no analytics or tracking SDKs, and never sell your data.

1. Who we are

Kirole (“Kirole”, “we”, “us”) makes a companion app for iPhone and a paired E-ink desk device. You can reach us any time at support@kirole.681023.xyz.

2. Information we handle

Account

You sign in with Sign in with Apple or Google Sign-In. We receive your name, email address, and a stable account identifier. We use these to create your account and to keep your companion's progress attached to it.

Connected calendars and tasks

Kirole works with the calendar and task sources you explicitly connect. The first App Store release supports Google Calendar, Google Tasks, Apple Calendar, and Apple Reminders. Apple sources are accessed on your device through EventKit. Connecting any source is optional; Google use is covered in section 3 and Apple use in section 4.

Companion and focus data

Your companion's state (character, level, energy bottles, unlocked scenes) and your focus-session history are stored on your iPhone. A small snapshot of companion status and sync state is backed up to our server so you can restore it if you move to a new phone. This snapshot does not contain your calendar events or tasks.

Custom companion photos

If you create a custom companion from a photo, the image is processed entirely on your device and converted to the E-ink art format there. The photo is never uploaded to us.

Location and local weather

If you allow location access while using the app, Kirole requests your current location to retrieve local weather from Apple Weather and to show a nearby place name. Kirole does not use location for advertising or tracking and does not store precise coordinates on our servers. You can deny or revoke location access in iOS Settings; the rest of the app remains available without local weather.

What we deliberately do not collect

  • No advertising identifiers and no ads.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.
  • No contacts or microphone data.
  • No use of location for advertising or cross-app tracking.

3. How we use Google user data

If you connect Google Calendar or Google Tasks, Kirole requests access to your calendar events and your task lists. Here is exactly what happens with that data:

  • Show your day. Events and tasks are displayed inside the Kirole app and on your paired E-ink device.
  • Let your companion respond to your day. When Kirole generates companion dialogue, day summaries, event categories, or short support text, relevant calendar or task content and timing may be sent to our AI text provider for that request. Kirole may also include relevant focus, goal, work-preference, and companion-setting context. We do not use Google user data to develop, improve, or train generalized artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.
  • Keep supported changes in step. When you edit a supported Google Calendar event or make a supported Google Tasks change in Kirole, the app sends that change to the corresponding Google service.

Your Google OAuth tokens are stored only in the iOS Keychain on your device. Your Google Calendar and Google Tasks content is not stored on our servers.

Kirole's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Other connected services

Apple Calendar and Apple Reminders are accessed on your device through EventKit and do not pass through our servers. Kirole reads the calendars and reminder lists you choose. When you perform an action that EventKit supports, the app may write that change back to the selected Apple calendar or reminder list. Relevant content may be sent to the AI provider only for the writing and classification requests described above.

5. Storage and security

  • Provider and account tokens used by the app are stored in the iOS Keychain rather than plain local storage.
  • Internet traffic uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • Our backend (a self-hosted Supabase instance) stores account information plus the companion and sync metadata described above; it does not store your calendar events or task content.
  • Communication with the E-ink device uses Bluetooth Low Energy and, for supported custom-avatar transfer, a temporary local Wi-Fi connection between your devices. This transfer is not routed through our servers.

6. Sharing

We do not sell personal data or share it with advertisers or data brokers. Depending on the features you choose, Kirole sends data to: Google for connected Calendar and Tasks requests; Apple system frameworks for on-device EventKit access and Apple services for local weather and place names; our AI text provider for the writing and classification requests described above; our hosting infrastructure for authentication and the account/companion/sync metadata described above; and your paired Kirole device for supported display and device operations. We may also disclose information if required by law.

7. Retention and deletion

  • Disconnect Google: disconnect Google in Kirole Settings to remove its app-held tokens from your device. You can control Apple Calendar, Reminders, and Location permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Revoke from Google's side: you can also revoke Kirole's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete local data: deleting the app removes all locally stored data, including tokens.
  • Delete your account: open Kirole Settings and tap Delete Account. This removes your server snapshot and the data stored on that iPhone. If you cannot open the app, email support@kirole.681023.xyz and we will complete deletion within 30 days.

8. Children

Kirole is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date above. Material changes will be announced inside the app.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy: support@kirole.681023.xyz.
